\section{System and simulation}
All three cases fit the \emph{same} mock lens (\texttt{shared/system\_config.py}, seed 87651):
an EPL+SHEAR deflector ($\theta_E=1.2''$, $e_1=0$, $e_2=0.1$, $\gamma=2$, shear $\gamma_1=0.1$,
$\gamma_2=0$) with a S\'ersic source at $(0.2,-0.05)''$ and a S\'ersic lens light, imaged on a
$40\times40$ grid at $0.1''$/px, Gaussian PSF (FWHM $0.067''$), background rms $10^{-2}$,
$t_{\rm exp}=2200$\,s. Peak SNR $\approx290$. The GW sector adds four lensed images with observables
= relative time delays (3) + effective luminosity distances (4); truth $T_\star=1.479\times10^7$\,s,
$d_L=11214$\,Mpc.

\textbf{Measurement-error regimes (GW sector).} Two settings are compared for the GW cases:
\emph{large-error} ($\sigma_{t_d}=5\%$, $\sigma_{d_L^{\rm eff}}=300\%$) and \emph{precise}
($\sigma_{t_d}=0.1\%$, $\sigma_{d_L^{\rm eff}}=5\%$). The simulated observables are identical;
only the assumed uncertainties (posterior widths) change. EM-only is unaffected.

\section{Case 1 --- EM-only}
\textbf{Free (11):} $\theta_E, e_1, e_2, \gamma, \gamma_1, \gamma_2$ and source
$A, R_{\rm S}, n, e_1, e_2$. \textbf{Fixed to truth:} lens + source centres, shear origin, full
lens-light profile, background noise. \textbf{Methods:} gwemfish deriv-approx (informed NUTS on a
Taylor model) and fisher (Taylor--Gaussian at truth); PyAutoLens Nautilus; lenstronomy Nautilus.
Priors: gwemfish/PAL use their native broad priors, lenstronomy uses truth $\pm10\sigma_{\rm Fisher}$
boxes; at SNR$\sim$290 the fit is data-dominated (verified by overlap).

\textbf{Result.} All four frameworks agree on every parameter to $\lesssim0.2\sigma$ in the means,
with widths matching to a few percent (Table~\ref{tab:c1}). The only outlier is source
amplitude/radius ($\sim$1$\sigma$, $\sim$0.2\%): the irreducible S\'ersic $b_n$-convention
difference (PAL Ciotti--Bertin vs.\ HCL/lenstronomy). Common $\sim$1--2$\sigma$ pulls
($\theta_E\,{-}1.2$, $n\,{+}2.1$) are the shared noise realization, not bias --- the truth-centred
Fisher posterior has identical widths.

\section{Case 2 --- GW-only}
\textbf{Free (4):} $e_2, \gamma, y_0^{\rm gw}, y_1^{\rm gw}$ (source position). \textbf{Fixed to
truth:} $\theta_E, e_1$, lens centre, all shear, $T_\star, d_L$. Constraint = time delays
($d_L^{\rm eff}$ nearly uninformative in large-error). \textbf{Methods:} fisher-source,
deriv-approx-source, nautilus-source (helens solver), and nautilus with a lenstronomy solver
(same GW likelihood, different lens-equation solver --- a cross-check).
\textbf{Priors (Uniform):} NUTS boxes $e_2\in(-0.5,0.5)$, $\gamma\in(1.5,2.5)$,
$y_0\in(0.1,0.3)$, $y_1\in(-0.13,0.03)$; nautilus uses truth\,$\pm3\sigma_{\rm Fisher}$ clipped to
these (tight in precise, NUTS-dominated in large-error).

\textbf{Result.} Every method is consistent with truth (all $|{\rm pull}|\le0.7$). Precise data
tighten the posteriors $\sim$5--40$\times$ (Table~\ref{tab:c2}) and \emph{resolve the $y_1^{\rm gw}$
bimodality} seen in large-error (Fig.~\ref{fig:c2}). Fisher-source flips from a poor,
$\sim$10$\times$-too-wide approximation (near-singular FM, cond $1.9\times10^9$) to an excellent one.
The two solvers agree to $\sim$$10^{-8}$ nats except on a $\sim$5--10\% caustic-boundary sliver each
truncates differently ($\le0.4\sigma$ mean shifts).

\section{Case 2f --- GW-only with $T_\star,d_L$ free: a degeneracy and its resolution}

\textbf{Motivation.} Case 2 fixes $T_\star$ (time-delay distance) and $d_L$ (luminosity
distance) to truth. Freeing them --- \textbf{free (6):} $e_2,\gamma,y_0^{\rm gw},y_1^{\rm gw},
T_\star,d_L$ --- tests whether GW-only data can measure the distance/time-delay normalisation
on their own, the quantity time-delay cosmography ultimately wants. \textbf{Methods:}
fisher-source, deriv-approx-source (regularized informed NUTS, required once $T_\star,d_L$ open
a near-flat direction), nautilus-source (helens solver), nautilus + lenstronomy solver.
\textbf{Priors:} Uniform truth$\pm50\%$ on $T_\star,d_L$; nautilus boxes from the fisher sigmas
(truth$\pm3\sigma$, clipped), as in Case 2.

\textbf{The degeneracy.} Freeing $T_\star,d_L$ opens two near-flat Fisher directions:
$\mathrm{corr}(d_L,\gamma)=-0.999$, $\mathrm{corr}(T_\star,y_1^{\rm gw})=+0.998$,
$\mathrm{corr}(T_\star,d_L)=-0.97$, with two unit-normalised Fisher eigenvalues at
$3.7\times10^{-11}$ and $1.1\times10^{-9}$ (verified real rather than inversion noise: sigmas
from the unit-normalised inversion match the fisher-meta sigmas to 4 decimal places).
Physically, $d_L^{\rm eff}=d_L/\sqrt{\mu}$ ties $d_L$ to the magnification, and time delays fix
only the Fermat-potential scale up to the $T_\star$ normalisation --- GW observables alone
under-determine which combination of $T_\star,d_L,\gamma$ produced the data.

\textbf{Precise regime ($\sigma_{t_d}=0.1\%,\sigma_{d_L^{\rm eff}}=5\%$): biased along the
ridge.} The two exact-likelihood samplers (helens and lenstronomy solvers) agree with each
other (means within $\sim0.5\sigma$) but both sit 1.3--3.1$\sigma$ down-ridge of truth
(Fig.~\ref{fig:c2f}a) --- a coherent shift along the degenerate direction, not a solver or
sampler bug (all six pulls move together with the correlation signs above). deriv-approx-source,
a Taylor surrogate centred at truth, stays truth-centred by construction and is the
local-curvature answer, not the full-posterior one.

\textbf{scan\_opt regime ($\sigma_{t_d}=1\%,\sigma_{d_L^{\rm eff}}=0.5\%$): resolved by
re-allocating error, not by shrinking it.} Because $d_L^{\rm eff}$, not $t_d$, carries the
distance normalisation, trading back 10$\times$ on time-delay precision to buy 10$\times$ on
$d_L^{\rm eff}$ collapses the ridge: every one of the 24 pulls across all four methods satisfies
$|p|\le0.62$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:c2f}b); $d_L$ is measured to $\sim$8--9\%, $T_\star$ to $\sim$4--5\%,
$\gamma$ to $\sim$10\%, source position to $\sim$2\%. This operating point was located in
advance with a Fisher error-requirement scan; its analytic $J^\top C^{-1}J$ prediction matches
the pipeline's measured Hessian sigmas to five decimal places at the chosen budget, confirming
the scan and the sampler compute the same object. fisher-source also becomes informative rather
than a correlation-only source: $\mathrm{cond}({\rm FM})$ improves from $\sim10^{26}$-class
degeneracy (precise) to $6.5\times10^{22}$ (scan\_opt).

\textbf{Caveats.} nautilus-helens has the fewest effective samples of the two exact solvers at
scan\_opt ($n_{\rm eff}=302$ vs 3023 for lenstronomy); its $\sim$0.5$\sigma$ residual pulls on
$\gamma,y_1^{\rm gw},T_\star$ are consistent with that sampling noise, not bias. $\gamma$ remains
largely prior-dominated at scan\_opt (GW-only constrains the $\gamma$--$d_L$ combination, not
$\gamma$ alone). The $T_\star$--$d_L$ ridge itself is not removed by scan\_opt, only bounded:
all three sampled methods still trace the same anti-correlated band, now centred on truth. This
degeneracy is exactly what Case 3 (EM+GW) breaks by an independent route --- pinning the
mass/source model with EM data rather than re-balancing GW error budgets.

\section{Case 3 --- EM+GW}
\textbf{Free (27):} full joint EM pixel model + GW sector ($T_\star, d_L, y_0^{\rm gw}, y_1^{\rm gw}$).
\textbf{Fixed to truth:} shear origin only. \textbf{Methods:} fisher-source, deriv-approx-source,
nautilus-source (helens). \textbf{Priors:} lens-light centroid $\mathcal{N}(0,0.05)$,
$y_0/y_1\sim$ Uniform truth$\pm0.05$, rest free per parameter layout; nautilus boxes truth
$\pm5\sigma_{\rm Fisher}$. (nautilus ties the GW source to the EM source centre, so its $y_0/y_1$
column carries the EM astrometric constraint.)

\textbf{Result.} The EM likelihood already pins the mass and source-position parameters, so
precise GW data change them negligibly; what they transform is the \emph{GW-only sector}
(Table~\ref{tab:c3}): $d_L$ goes from effectively unconstrained ($\pm11800$, $\sim$70\%) to
$\pm288$\,Mpc ($\sim$2.6\%), and $T_\star$ tightens $\sim$11$\times$. fisher and nautilus recover
truth (nautilus $|{\rm pull}|\le1.2$; large pulls are std-amplification, not bias --- absolute
accuracy is sub-mas). \emph{Caveat:} deriv-approx (banana surrogate) mixes poorly on the sharp
precise 27-D posterior (worst $\hat R\approx1.9$); treat its contour as indicative --- fisher and
nautilus are the reliable methods here.

\section{Conclusion}
On a common mock, independent codes and inference methods agree:
(i) \textbf{EM-only} --- gwemfish, PyAutoLens and lenstronomy recover truth to $\lesssim0.2\sigma$,
the only residual being the known S\'ersic $b_n$-convention offset;
(ii) \textbf{GW-only} --- all methods are truth-consistent; precise timing shrinks the posterior
$\sim$5--40$\times$ and resolves the $y_1^{\rm gw}$ bimodality, and Fisher becomes an accurate
surrogate;
(iii) \textbf{GW-only, $T_\star,d_L$ free} --- freeing the distance/time-delay normalisation opens
a near-perfect $T_\star$--$d_L$--$\gamma$ degeneracy (correlations $\ge0.97$) that biases every
method coherently by 1.3--3.1$\sigma$ under a time-delay-optimised error budget; re-allocating the
same total measurement effort toward $d_L^{\rm eff}$ precision (found in advance with a Fisher
error-requirement scan) collapses all 24 pulls to $|p|\le0.62$ and yields $d_L$ to $\sim$8--9\%,
$T_\star$ to $\sim$4--5\%, with zero extra data --- the key lesson being that \emph{how} error
budget is spent matters as much as its total size, and a tight posterior is not necessarily a
correct one;
(iv) \textbf{EM+GW} --- EM fixes the lens/source, so precise GW data act almost entirely on the
GW-only sector, turning $d_L$ from unconstrained into a $\sim$2.6\% measurement --- an independent
route to breaking the same $T_\star$--$d_L$--$\gamma$ degeneracy that (iii) resolves by GW error
re-allocation alone.
Cross-checks hold throughout (independent solvers agree to $\sim$$10^{-8}$ nats; vectorized vs.\
scalar likelihoods to $\sim$$10^{-11}$; analytic Fisher predictions match measured Hessian sigmas
to 5 decimal places). The one method-level caveat is deriv-approx-source, whose banana-surrogate
NUTS mixes poorly once a posterior becomes very sharp or opens a near-flat direction without
regularization --- fisher-source and nautilus-source remain reliable across all regimes tested.

This concludes the current phase of the comparison analysis: EM-only, GW-only (fixed and free
$T_\star,d_L$, three error regimes), and EM+GW are all cross-validated across gwemfish, PyAutoLens
and lenstronomy on one common mock, with every run's samples, priors and configuration saved for
exact reproduction (see \texttt{comparison-analysis/*/results*.md} and \texttt{handoff.md}).
Open follow-ups (not required for this conclusion) are recorded in
\texttt{comparison-analysis/handoff.md}: a mac-environment (herculens 0.3.x) rerun to confirm
absolute numbers, and a Case 3 nautilus-source variant with a freed GW source position.
